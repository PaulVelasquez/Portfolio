About Me

A quick introduction

I'm Paul John Velasquez — a software engineer, entrepreneur, and CTO based in the Philippines. I co-founded Swiftly Workspace, an AI-native ERP platform, and lead the technical vision behind three products: Swiftly Workspace, FeedbackKit, and Swiftly AI.

My background spans backend development, software testing, freelance engineering, and virtual assistance — giving me a well-rounded perspective on building products from the ground up. I'm passionate about leveraging AI and modern tooling to solve real business problems.
